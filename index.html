How we work

Built for buyers who are under pressure

A stopped line does not care which brand the sensor is. Our job is to get the right part specified, quoted and delivered before the shift changes.

Quoted the same day

Despatched across India

About

A supply house built around the maintenance engineer

Maven Works was established in Chennai in 2023 as a partnership firm trading in industrial automation products. We supply the sensors, instrumentation and control components that keep manufacturing and process plants running.

Our position

Independent, and useful because of it

Most plants run a mix of brands. Standards change, machines arrive from different builders, and an obsolete part rarely has a like-for-like replacement from the same maker. A buyer in that position does not need a single-brand salesperson — they need someone who knows the range across makers and will say plainly what will work.

That is the position we have built: multi-brand, technically literate, and quick. We would rather lose an order by recommending a cheaper equivalent than win one that fails in service.
